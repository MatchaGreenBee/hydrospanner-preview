Privacy

Effective [date].

Hydrospanner LLC runs this website. This page explains what we collect through it and what we do with it.

What we collect. When you send the contact form, we collect your name, email, company if you give one, the service you pick, your message, and the time you sent it. Like most websites, our host, Google Cloud, keeps standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to run and secure the site. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

Spam check. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that the sender is a person. Cloudflare handles the data that check needs under its own privacy policy.

How we use it. We use it only to read and answer your inquiry and to keep a record of our conversation. We don't sell or share your information, and sending the form never adds you to a marketing list.

Who receives it. The two of us who run Hydrospanner LLC, plus the providers that run our systems on our behalf: Google for hosting and email, Cloudflare for the spam check, and Anthropic, whose AI models help us sort incoming messages.

Tracking. We don't track visitors over time or across other sites, and we don't let advertising or analytics companies do so here. We treat every visit the same, whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

How long we keep it. We keep your inquiry as long as we need it to work with you and to keep business records. If you ask, we'll delete it unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices. Email [address] to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Changes. If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date.
